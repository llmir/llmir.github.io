At CATL HKRI, my research spans Agentic AI and AI for Science. I study how agents improve through experience and how AI can advance materials discovery and battery characterization.

  • Recursive self-improvement (RSI) and automated agent harness optimization. I study how agents can turn recurring failures into lasting improvements. My work combines trace-guided harness optimization with multi-round evaluations of cross-task transfer and capability retention.
  • Multi-agent AutoResearch frameworks. I study how agents can carry out iterative scientific inquiry, coordinating hypothesis development, experiment design, and independent verification so that results can refine subsequent hypotheses (machine-learned force fields, materials property prediction and screening, etc.).
  • Multimodal, generalizable AI for battery characterization. I combine advanced signal processing and multimodal AI to characterize battery state and microstructure from complementary measurements, including ultrasound and scanning electron microscopy (SEM), across cells and acquisition conditions.

My interest in AI for Healthcare grew out of undergraduate research in biomedical engineering. I began working on medical imaging during my master’s studies and made it the focus of my PhD at the University of Hong Kong (doctoral thesis). I continue to develop learning methods that address challenges shared across medical image analysis tasks.

  • Data- and annotation-efficient learning. I study how to learn when expert labels are scarce and supervision is incomplete or imperfect, drawing on a range of approaches (self-supervised, weakly supervised, semi-supervised, active, and noise-robust learning, etc.).
  • Generalizable AI and foundation models. I develop multimodal representations that can transfer across imaging protocols, institutions, and tasks, and study how domain generalization and efficient adaptation can support deployment in new settings.
  • Personalized federated learning. I develop federated optimization and model personalization methods that learn across institutional data silos while accounting for differences in data distributions and supervision across sites.

For collaborations in Agentic AI, recursive self-improvement (RSI), AI for Science, AI for Healthcare, computer vision, multimodal AI, or related areas, feel free to email me or connect on WeChat. In your WeChat request, please briefly introduce yourself and mention what you’d like to discuss.
