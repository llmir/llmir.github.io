The University of Hong Kong

Sep 2021 – Sep 2025

Doctoral Researcher, OMEGA Lab · Joint PhD training with SUSTech MedICAL Lab

Data- and annotation-efficient learning

I studied how to recover dense segmentation from sparse, noisy supervision. YoloCurvSeg separates the synthesis of curvilinear anatomy from the reconstruction of real image backgrounds and uses contrastive learning to combine them. Its single-skeleton setting used 0.03–1.40% of the fully annotated pixels across four benchmarks. ProCNS uses prototype calibration and region-level noise suppression to limit the accumulation of pseudo-label errors during training.

YoloCurvSegYoloCurvSeg · ProCNSProCNS · LF-SynthSegLF-SynthSeg

Cross-domain generalization and adaptation

My work separates shifts in the input distribution from changes in what a site annotates. AADG searches for augmentation policies that improve generalization to unseen imaging domains. UniOCTSeg++ uses hierarchical prompts and bidirectional consistency to share supervision across annotation granularities. On its external OCT test sets, training with 30% labeled data plus unlabeled scans yielded a Dice score of 86.26%, versus 86.83% with full supervision. I have also worked on parameter-efficient adaptation for high-resolution imaging.

AADGAADG · UniOCTSeg++UniOCTSeg++ · Fusion Side TuningFusion Side Tuning

Personalized federated learning across data silos

I developed federated methods for institutions whose scans and weak labels follow different distributions. FedICRA learns site-aware representations and adapts the aggregation of segmentation heads. FedLPPA adds prompts for each site’s data distribution and annotation sparsity, together with a dual decoder and parameter-wise aggregation. Its evaluation covered four segmentation tasks across fundus imaging, OCTA, endoscopy and MRI.

FedLPPAFedLPPA · FedICRAFedICRA · Doctoral thesis

Multimodal foundation models for medical image analysis

I work on reusable medical image representations from unlabeled scans, paired reports and complementary imaging sequences. Uni4Eye++ uses entropy-guided masking and modality-specific reconstruction heads to pretrain jointly on 2D and 3D scans. PRIOR aligns images and reports at global and local levels and evaluates transfer on five downstream tasks. In brain MRI, MF-MCM learns cross-sequence complementarity to support lesion segmentation when annotations or modalities are missing.

Uni4Eye++Uni4Eye++ · PRIORPRIOR · MF-MCMMF-MCM

Image analysis in clinical workflows

SynLiTS combines phase-conditioned synthesis with context-aware fusion for imperfectly aligned liver CT. MEFN estimates uncertainty before combining PET and CT evidence. FOCUS adapts a vision foundation model to analyze OCT slices and aggregates their predictions for patient-level diagnosis. The study evaluated the diagnostic workflow at four external centers.
